# Backlinks — realistic outreach list and templates

Principles: links from pages that would mention ETAHG anyway (own group site, chambers, directories, press, suppliers, associations). One listing per platform. No paid links, link farms, "free directory" networks, reciprocal-link pages or DA sellers. Every outgoing e-mail uses the NAP strings from `NAP.md`. The owner decides before any supplier or dealer is named (the website itself never names machine brands; the link direction is supplier → ETAHG).
