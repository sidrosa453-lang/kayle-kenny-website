## 3. About (≤ 2,000 chars; LinkedIn supports one text per language — add EN as default, FR and ZH as translations under Admin → Page info → "Manage languages")

**EN**
`SARL ETAHG is an Algerian company established in 1997 and registered in Ghardaïa (full name: Entreprise de Travaux d'Aménagement Hydraulique de Ghardaïa). It is specialised in fine aggregate production, road construction, heavy equipment rental and leasing, project mobilization and water well drilling.

The company operates its own quarry and stone crushing plant at Oued Sdeur, Aïn El Ibel (wilaya of Djelfa), producing high-quality fine aggregates suited to projects that require high production rates. Its equipment depot is in Djelfa, on the RN1 axis linking Algiers to the Sahara, which places ETAHG between the north and the south of the country.

The fleet — hydraulic excavators, bulldozer, wheel loaders, motor graders, asphalt paver, vibratory compactors, trucks and tipper semi-trailers, bitumen distributor and tankers, a surface rock-drilling carriage and a truck-mounted water well drilling rig — was built up for road construction in many regions of Algeria and is now also offered for rent and lease. Terms (duration, operators, transport, maintenance) are agreed per project.

ETAHG works as a local partner, subcontractor or supplier for EPC and infrastructure contractors, mining and cement companies, equipment manufacturers and investors, as well as for Algerian public and private project owners. The group includes EURL KAYLE KENNY (Mohammadia, Algiers), a supplier of heavy-duty diesel engine spare parts, Cummins-compatible — independent supplier, not affiliated with or endorsed by Cummins Inc.

Website in English, French, Arabic and Chinese: https://www.etahg.com/ — Contact: +213 558 96 10 49 (WhatsApp) · contact@etahg.com`
