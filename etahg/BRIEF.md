## Hard rules for content

1. **Never invent** numbers or specifics: no founding year, no fleet counts, no
   tonnes/hour, no km of roads, no project names, no client names, no wilaya
   names claimed as project locations, no certifications (ISO…), no awards, no
   staff counts, no brand names of machines. These exist as fields in
   `site.config.json`; the templates render them **only when filled**.
2. General, truthful domain explanation is encouraged (what fine aggregates are
   used for, how a crushing circuit works, what a mobilization involves, how
   Algeria's terrain types — coastal Tell, Atlas mountains, High Plateaus,
   Sahara — affect road building and drilling). Phrase it as expertise/know-how,
   not as claims of specific past projects in specific places.
3. Rental terms (duration, operators, transport, maintenance) are "agreed per
   project" — do not promise specific terms.
4. Tone: corporate, confident, precise, international. No hype words
   ("world-class", "#1", "best in Algeria"), no emoji.
5. Every page must be useful on its own to a reader who knows nothing about
   ETAHG — AI engines quote pages in isolation.
6. Kayle Kenny disclaimer must be kept wherever Cummins is mentioned:
   "independent supplier, not affiliated with or endorsed by Cummins Inc."
